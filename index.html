How JEBI Capital Works

A crowdfunding platform purpose-built for the zero-carbon coffee economy.

For Creators

Turn your climate-positive coffee idea into a funded reality.

1

Apply

Submit your project with a carbon reduction plan. Our team reviews within 3–5 days.

2

Verify

Get your carbon impact verified by our accredited third-party partners.

3

Launch

Go live and start raising funds from a global community of climate-conscious backers.

4

Deliver

Execute your project, send rewards, and report carbon impact quarterly.

Back projects that decarbonize coffee. Earn rewards, equity, or carbon credits.

1

Discover

Browse verified projects across the coffee supply chain.

2

Pledge

Choose a reward tier or invest in equity. Every project has verified carbon impact.

3

Track

Monitor project progress and carbon offset in real-time through your dashboard.

4

Receive

Get your rewards, dividends, or carbon credits as projects reach milestones.

The Standard Deal

One transparent term for every team — no negotiation, no exceptions. That's the point.

01

$50k–150k on a SAFE

Standardized investment for 5–7% equity, based on stage. Same term for every team in the batch.

02

Aeroponic technology license

Access to NAPELL aeroponic cultivation systems, smart-farm IoT stack, and propagation know-how.

03

Carbon verification included

Third-party verification (Verra, Gold Standard, SCS) of your project's carbon impact, covered by JEBI.

04

Route to market

Distribution through the JEBI ecosystem: farms, roasteries, cafés, and the SHINAE Coffee Bank platform.

12 Weeks, Then Demo Day

Remote-first with an on-farm residency. Built to compress years of learning into weeks.

Weeks 1–2

Selection & onboarding — finalize the deal, define your coffee-chain track, set carbon baselines.

Weeks 3–6

Build — product, supply contracts, aeroponic pilots, and customer conversations. Weekly check-ins.

Weeks 7–10

On-farm residency — hands-on aeroponic training and supply-chain integration at a partner base.

Weeks 11–12

Demo Day prep — pitch rehearsal, metrics, and introductions to our investor and partner network.

Demo Day

Present to a curated room of climate investors, coffee trade buyers, and strategic partners. Batch projects also get priority placement on the JEBI Capital crowdfunding platform.

Three Ways In

We select teams across every stage of the coffee value chain — the batch becomes a supply chain.

01 Supply side

Cultivation, processing, and origin operations. Get aeroponic tech, verification, and offtake partners.

02 Demand side

Roasting, cafés, and retail. Secure verified low-carbon supply and sell through the JEBI network.

03 Finance & carbon

Fintech, carbon MRV, and market infrastructure. Plug into SHINAE Coffee Bank and BeanFlow.

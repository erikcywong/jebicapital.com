Every project on JEBI Capital undergoes rigorous third-party carbon verification.

📏

Baseline Assessment

Project's current carbon footprint is measured against industry benchmarks.

🔍

Reduction Plan Review

Our accredited verifiers assess the feasibility of the proposed carbon reduction.

📡

Ongoing Monitoring

IoT sensors and satellite data track actual carbon savings in real-time.

📋

Annual Audit

Independent annual audits ensure carbon claims remain accurate and verifiable.
